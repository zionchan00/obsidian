- There's something about the repetition of rituals by descendants without knowledge of why the rituals were done in the first place, like the practice of astrology.
- Imagine yourself as one of the first of men to walk this Earth. Over the course of your life you notice change in the things around you and in yourself. You begun searching for a cause for this change and you have to notice that certain changes are correlated, suggesting that one may cause the other.
- You begin with the most noticeable changes. Day and night comes with sun and moon and if one lives by the sea, they will notice how the movements of the moon affects the waves around them. Broadly it seems the heavenly bodies are the only beings which can cause movement; all else is subject to the heavenly bodies.
- One of the first men must have noticed that actually there were other creatures who were capable of manipulating other objects - living creatures. If the heavenly bodies and living creatures are the only things which can cause movement, perhaps that which makes one alive - the soul - comes from the stars.
- This is the original belief which supported the ritual of astrology. Explaining the implications of this origin story to 'soft' believers who casually drop horoscopes as explanations of phenomena - that to believe in astrology is to believe that every one of us has a soul from space - there's a high chance they'll drop the practice once they realize how different the logic is from their system of metaphysics.
