- scales and chords
	- Nick mira's tip: go up 7 semitones from any random note. and if you want to make a major, go up 4 from the starting and press all 3 of these keys at once to make the chord, and if you want a minor press 3 semitones up from minor
		- And if you go up 7 semitones from your 2nd note, then you have a 4 note chord. Making it major or minor by changing the 2nd note and the resulting 4th note
		- He also likes to sometimes take the middle note and make it go up 7 semitones for the chord
	- intervals can be major or minor, with a numbere. e
	- chords have to have 3 notes at least. key is determined by the "middle" note whcih akes it major or minor
	-  WWHWWH for major, WHWHWW
	- Pentatonic tones
	- whole tone is when you make a scale of only whole ascending notes
	- The octatonic or diminished scale is an eight note scale in which the intervals alternate between whole and half steps. This scale is sometimes used in jazz.
	- diatonic triad = forming a chord by using every other note in a specific scale. this means there are triads which don't use only notes from a said scale. in other words non-diatonic means you make chords with notes that aren't in key
	- INversion of a triad = you chnage what note is at the base
	- there are chord progressions
		- parallel = across the chords, same distance betwene notes, just different keys
