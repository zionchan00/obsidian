**Essential Ericksonian Hypnosis Books** 

- **My Voice Will Go With You: The Teaching Tales of Milton H. Erickson** (edited by Sidney Rosen)  
    This is the most accessible entry point. It’s a collection of stories and therapeutic anecdotes that demonstrate Erickson’s indirect, story-based approach. If you don’t get the point after reading this, you’re probably not cut out for the style. [​⁠](https://mikemandelhypnosis.com/hypnosis-training/ten-books-that-every-hypnotist-needs-to-read/)
- **Patterns of the Hypnotic Techniques of Milton H. Erickson, M.D. (Volumes I & II)** by Richard Bandler & John Grinder  
    These are foundational texts for understanding the structure of Erickson’s methods. They’re dense, sometimes meandering, but if you want to see how NLP cannibalized Erickson, this is the place. [​⁠](https://www.reddit.com/r/hypnosis/comments/ek3xpn/where_to_start_with_milton_erickson_and_general/)
- **Hypnotic Realities: The Induction of Clinical Hypnosis and Forms of Indirect Suggestion** by Milton H. Erickson & Ernest Rossi  
    This is as close as you’ll get to a technical manual from the man himself. It’s not “how-to” in the YouTube sense, but it’s the real deal. [​⁠](https://ericksonian.com/books-suggested-reading-list)
- **A Teaching Seminar with Milton H. Erickson** by Jeffrey Zeig  
    If you want to see how Erickson actually taught, this is the closest you’ll get to sitting in the room. [​⁠](https://www.reddit.com/r/hypnosis/comments/ek3xpn/where_to_start_with_milton_erickson_and_general/)
- **Taproots: Underlying Principles of Milton Erickson’s Therapy and Hypnosis** by William O’Hanlon  
    This is a meta-level book that tries to distill the “why” behind the “how.” It’s not a script book, but it’s useful for understanding the philosophy. [​⁠](https://ericksonian.com/books-suggested-reading-list)
- **The Collected Papers of Milton H. Erickson** (4 volumes)  
    This is for the completist or the masochist. It’s clinical, sprawling, and not for beginners, but it’s the archive. [​⁠](https://mikemandelhypnosis.com/hypnosis-training/ten-books-that-every-hypnotist-needs-to-read/)

**Secondary/Contextual Reads** 

- **Uncommon Therapy** by Jay Haley  
    A classic outsider’s perspective on Erickson’s work. It’s more narrative than technical, but it’s a good way to see how others interpreted his methods. [​⁠](https://ericksonian.com/books-suggested-reading-list)
- **The Answer Within: A Clinical Framework of Ericksonian Hypnotherapy** by Stephen Lankton  
    A more structured, clinical approach to the framework. [​⁠](https://www.amazon.ca/Answer-Within-Framework-Ericksonian-Hypnotherapy/dp/1845901215)
- **Monsters and Magical Sticks: There’s No Such Thing as Hypnosis?** by Steven Heller  
    Not strictly Ericksonian, but it’s a good bridge between the old school and the new
