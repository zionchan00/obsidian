## Highlights
- The city whispers to its denizens at all times of the day. Aware or unaware, we are driven to desire what the city demands we desire. In this way the soul of the urban man is crafted by forces beyond his comprehension.
  Some days, the whispers are merely an intrusive murmur riding on the wind.
  ‘Do you see what he has? Desire it, chase it, and it is yours.’ 
  ‘Let go of the past; the future is here.’ 
  ‘You can be so much more than you are.’
  Other days, the city’s whispers crescendo into a silent cacophony of screams rendering you an immobile wreck as the pitiful record of your life plays with a sad little violin in the background.
  ‘Why do you play at these games? Your peers have achieved more than you. You are a relic of the past. You don’t have what it takes to survive here.’
  Every city whispers its own set of secrets and its values. The city is a machine of cultural production unlike any other created by man. It swallows the rustic and demands refinement to a fault, driving the mimetic competition between men to prove that they are better than their peers; they are the urban sophisticates; they make the city, and the city makes them ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01gt0wjt1gw0pkz2z2bg7wmfc5))
