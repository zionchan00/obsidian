## Highlights
- But when there isn’t slack in the system, a small incremental increase in final demand can produce *massive* changes in total production capacity. The rough way to approximate this is to look at the useful life of the relevant investment, invert it into a depreciation rate, and then compare changes in demand to that depreciation rate. So if there's some kind of asset that lasts for 10 years, another way to look at it is that in a given year, 10% of those assets are getting replaced as they wear out. A 2% increase in demand for whatever those assets produce, if they're all being used at full capacity, means a *20%* increase in demand for the assets.
