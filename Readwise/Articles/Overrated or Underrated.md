## Highlights
- Mises is *underrated*.  His 1920 book [Socialism](https://www.amazon.com/Socialism-Economic-Sociological-Analysis-Enlarged/dp/1614272999/ref=sr_1_1?crid=YNA4442B38LH&keywords=mises+socialism&qid=1665887368&qu=eyJxc2MiOiIyLjI2IiwicXNhIjoiMS42OCIsInFzcCI6IjEuNzIifQ%3D%3D&sprefix=mises+socialism%2Caps%2C122&sr=8-1) is still the best and also historically most important critique of socialism, ever.  His earlier [articles](https://www.amazon.com/Collectivist-Economic-Planning-F-Hayek/dp/1610161629/ref=sr_1_1?crid=ODDAY06XLEMD&keywords=collectivist+economic+planning&qid=1665887400&qu=eyJxc2MiOiIwLjc5IiwicXNhIjoiMC44MSIsInFzcCI6IjAuNzIifQ%3D%3D&sprefix=collectivist+economic+planning%2Caps%2C103&sr=8-1) about the impossibility of economic calculation under socialism are among the most important economics articles, ever.  Those are already some pretty important contributions, and yet he is often talked of as a crank, perhaps because in part some of his followers were indeed cranks ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01gfkw04erm12yfxf09j0v44mr))
