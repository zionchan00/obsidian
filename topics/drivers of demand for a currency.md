Payment - the more entities accept the acceptance of a currency as a method of payment for goods/services, the higher the demand for a currency
Store of value - a currency is a good store of value when the price is low in volatility, which decreases the chances that your savings will be wiped. Ideally, it's also hard to steal and able to escape physical decay.
Intrinsic value - a currency can be worth the amount that 'backs' it. I.e if you have $100 each dollar could be exchanged for a ton of gold, then each dollar is worth a ton of gold. I'm not sure what my old view is, but One thing Defi has hammered into me is the framing of value as an exchange; the value of something is dependent on what you can trade it for, the conversion of one measurement of value to another way to measure value. 
Investment asset - the belief that the demand for a currency in the future will be higher than it is today either because a) demand will increase such that demand is greater than supply or b) supply will decrease such that supply is less than demand will drive demand for the currency today. This is something most users of a currency don't think of every day but drives much of the demand for a currency in crypto
