Big difference is that we go from 'these features of the concept ALWAYS have to occur together' to 'features TEND' from classical theory
Pros
- categorization
Problems
- Still suffers from the empiricist problem when explaining concept acquisition, but this can be worked around through non-empiricist versions
- Typically doesn't necessarily mean we have to have a prototype structure, bc even well-defined concepts where we don't 'statistically weigh' things have typicality effects
- Ignorance and error just like classical theory. non-instances can be accidentally included and atypical ones tend to be missed too
- Some concepts don't have prototypes. E.g justice, lie
- Compositionality. Fuzzy set theory and selective modification mdoel don't solve this either
