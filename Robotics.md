	- Robotics has none of these advantages. There is no internet-scale corpus of manipulation trajectories. Every hour of useful data has to be physically produced by a human being doing a task in a real environment. And the field hasn’t converged on what “useful” means yet. The main bottleneck in robotics data is not volume and not even quality in the traditional sense. It’s that there is no prevailing model modality. The field is betting heavy on video, but that’s about all anyone can say with confidence. Standardizing collection around RGB plus IMU will help, but if the winning architecture ends up requiring something else entirely, then all the collection infrastructure built around unsophisticated data players’ perceptions of a dominant data modality will be useless. Unless you have the same device circulating millions of times around the world, and unless that device turns out to be the correct one, you do not actually know whether your collection is high quality or correct. The diversity requirement makes convergence harder, not easier.

This creates a vendor evaluation problem that doesn’t really exist in text. In text-based data markets, the spec is relatively stable today - you need human experts who can produce reasoning traces, evaluate model outputs, or craft reward signals. The modality is language. The tooling is a browser and a rubric. You can swap vendors without fundamentally changing what data you’re collecting. In robotics, the vendor choice is entangled with the hardware choice, which is entangled with the modality choice, which is entangled with an unsettled research question about what models actually need. Switching vendors can mean switching sensor rigs, recalibrating annotation pipelines, and throwing away months of collection. If I’m Physical Intelligence or Dyna Robotics, why would I develop a long term relationship with a robotics data vendor, knowing that my specs (compared to the rest of the field) demand infrastructure understanding too bespoke for a robotics data vendor to adapt their entire infrastructure around me for?
