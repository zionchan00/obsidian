Enterprise startups are collapsing in vision, slogan, and hiring needs. Every kid I meet who's 19-23 is super excited to velocity-slop and grind, telling me who it's awesome to be so *high leverage*, but everyone I know around 25-28 or older just seems tired and like they're barely going.

This is the difference between people who seriously believe they're increasing the GDP of the internet at Stripe vs the people who are there nad think about it as just a job. This is the difference beetwen the Palantir employees a decade ago who earnestly believed that the company had no sales people or marketers and hence the value of the company was entirely dependent on 'the value of the product'.  This is the difference between a guy who tells people he's in privae equity as a 'search fund operator' and you can see his shoulders stiffen and his chest goes up as he says it vs the kind of person who looks at this and realizes it's status arbitrage.

The more I thought about it and the more companies I was meeting in the Bay, I realized, holy fuck you are all totally bought into some manic mania. Your eyes are crazy eyes. Your voices are religious. You're repeating some mantra that your CEO came up with while trying to fundraise as if you repeat it enough times I'll fall for it too. 

I'm still new relatively new to this whole tech shit and 'working a real job'. But one of the worst things about tech nowadays is the online memeplex makes it incredibly easy for a young person to fall for all of the above and more. And this isn't to say that there aren't companies working on moral missions, or that there aren't companies who will make you a minted multi-millionare, or anything like that. People here will write very romantically about 'the founder' or 'the mission'. Yes those things are *real*. But you cannot do anything but supplant cold analysis of the trade you are getting from you and the firm. Recognize that all of these things don't hide the fact that the firm is getting something from you and you're supposed to get something back.
